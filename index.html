About VGEC

Vishwakarma Government Engineering College fosters a vibrant culture of innovation, creativity and entrepreneurship, where students are encouraged to think beyond boundaries and transform ideas into impactful solutions. The institute provides a dynamic environment that promotes research, collaboration and problem-solving through modern learning practices and technical excellence.

Department of Chemical Engineering

The Chemical Engineering Department at VGEC promotes a strong culture of innovation, research and practical learning, encouraging students to develop sustainable and industry-oriented solutions for real-world challenges. Through advanced laboratories, technical training, research activities and collaborative projects, students gain hands-on experience and analytical skills essential for modern chemical engineering.
